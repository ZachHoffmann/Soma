The Physiological Sigh
Use when butterflies, nausea, or sudden heat hit. Two nasal inhales followed by one prolonged mouth sigh.
1
Take a deep nasal breath filling ~80% of your lungs.
2
Without exhaling, take a second sharp "sip" of air to fully expand alveoli.
3
Release a slow, soft sigh through your mouth until your lungs are completely empty.

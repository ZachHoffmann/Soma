1. The Physiological Sigh
Rapidly down-regulates autonomic arousal when heart rate spikes or panic loops begin.
1
Deep nasal breath filling ~80% capacity.
2
Second sharp "sip" of air to fully reinflate lung alveoli.
3
Long, unforced mouth sigh until lungs are completely empty.
2. Abdominal Unclamping (Gut Release)
Use when feeling butterflies, stomach flips, or nausea. Releases somatic muscular constriction around the celiac (solar) plexus.
1
Hand to Navel: Place one flat hand directly over your lower stomach. Notice if your core muscles are gripping inward.
2
Drop the Guard: Consciously soften your belly completely outward into your hand. Un-clench your jaw and let your tongue fall from the roof of your mouth.
3
Slow Belly Expansions: Take 3 slow nasal breaths where only your belly pushes your hand out. Chest and shoulders stay completely still.
3. Cold Thermal Reset (Dive Reflex)
Use when feeling sudden heat, hot flashes, or acute cognitive overwhelm.
1
Run cold tap water over the insides of both wrists for 30–60 seconds, or press a cold damp towel across your forehead and eyes.
2
The sudden temperature shift activates trigeminal nerve receptors, triggering the mammalian dive reflex to drop heart rate immediately.
